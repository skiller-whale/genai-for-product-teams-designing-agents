# Pod Membership

Our annual membership scheme for regulars. Three tiers, named — like everything else here — after the sea.

## Barnacle — £20/year

- 5% off all tour bookings.
- Members' newsletter, *The Monthly Spout*.

## Dolphin — £45/year

- 10% off all tour bookings.
- One **free reschedule** per season: move any one booking to a new date at no charge, any time up to departure.
- Members' newsletter.

## Orca — £90/year

- 15% off all tour bookings.
- **Refund floor**: when an Orca member cancels a booking, their refund is never less than 50% of the amount paid, regardless of how little notice they give. Where the standard refund ladder would give more (for example 75% or 100%), the ladder applies as normal.
- **Priority rebooking** after weather cancellations: Orca members are offered places on replacement sailings before general release.
- Members' newsletter, plus an invitation to the end-of-season Fluke Ball.

## How discounts work

- The membership discount applies to the tour price at the time of booking.
- Membership discounts can be combined with gift vouchers and re-sail vouchers (the discount is applied first, then vouchers are deducted).
- Membership discounts cannot be combined with promo codes: a booking uses one or the other, whichever the customer prefers.
- Membership benefits apply only to bookings where the member is one of the named passengers.
