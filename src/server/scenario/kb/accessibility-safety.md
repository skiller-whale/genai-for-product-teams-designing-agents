# Accessibility & Safety

## Age limits

- Harbour Hop: all ages (children under 3 sail free on a lap).
- Half-Day Humpback: ages 5 and over.
- Full-Day Orca Odyssey: ages 12 and over.
- Whale Song Silent Retreat: ages 16 and over.
- Midnight Bioluminescence Cruise: ages 18 and over.

## Wheelchair access

- **The Salty Fluke** and **Brinehilde** are both wheelchair accessible, with roll-on boarding ramps and accessible toilets.
- **The Wandering Molly** is a historic wooden sailing vessel and is, regrettably, not wheelchair accessible: boarding is by a stepped gangway and the deck has raised sills throughout.

## Pregnancy

Open-water tours (everything except the Harbour Hop) are not recommended beyond 24 weeks of pregnancy, because of unpredictable swell.
The Harbour Hop, in sheltered water, is fine at any stage — but please talk to your midwife if unsure.

## Safety on board

- Lifejackets are provided and must be worn on deck at all times on all tours; child sizes are available.
- All vessels carry a qualified first-aider and are in radio contact with Port Brine harbour control throughout.
- Follow crew instructions at all times; the skipper's word is final.
- We do not give medical advice. For questions about seasickness medication, pregnancy, or existing conditions, please talk to a pharmacist or your GP before sailing.
