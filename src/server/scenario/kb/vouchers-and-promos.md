# Vouchers, Gift Vouchers & Promo Codes

Three different things, three different sets of rules. Please don't mix them up — our till certainly won't.

## Gift vouchers

- Sold in any amount from £10 to £500.
- Valid for 24 months from purchase.
- Redeemable against any tour, membership, or harbour-shop purchase.
- Can be combined with membership discounts and with re-sail vouchers.

## Re-sail vouchers

- Issued under the Sighting Guarantee, or occasionally as goodwill.
- One free place on the named tour, valid until the end of the current season (31 October).
- Personal to the named customer; not transferable, not redeemable for cash.
- Can be used alongside membership discounts and gift vouchers.

## Promo codes

- Issued in marketing campaigns (e.g. SPLASH10 for 10% off).
- **One promo code per booking.**
- **Cannot be combined** with membership discounts, gift vouchers, or re-sail vouchers: a promo code booking is priced from the standard rate only.
- Not valid during Festival Week (20–26 July).
- Have an expiry date printed in the campaign material; expired codes cannot be honoured, however nicely the customer asks.

## Order of calculation

When several reductions apply to one booking, the till applies them in this order:

1. Start from the standard tour price (or group/school rate where applicable).
2. Apply the membership discount **or** promo code (never both).
3. Add the Festival surcharge, if the sailing is in Festival Week.
4. Deduct gift vouchers and re-sail vouchers last.
