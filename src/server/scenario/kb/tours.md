# Tours & Prices

The Barnacle & Fluke Whale-Watching Company runs five regular tours out of Port Brine harbour.
All prices are per person in GBP.

## Harbour Hop

- Duration: 1 hour
- Price: £25 adult, £10 child (ages 3–15)
- Vessel: The Salty Fluke
- Season: year-round
- A gentle loop of Port Brine harbour and the seal haul-out at Gullet Rock. Suitable for all ages. Note: this tour visits harbour seals and seabirds — whale sightings are rare this close to shore.

## Half-Day Humpback

- Duration: 3 hours
- Price: £60 adult, £24 child (ages 5–15)
- Vessel: Brinehilde
- Season: March–October
- Our most popular tour. Out past the Brine Ledges to the humpback feeding grounds.

## Full-Day Orca Odyssey

- Duration: 6 hours
- Price: £120 (ages 12 and over only)
- Vessel: Brinehilde
- Season: May–September
- A full day tracking the resident orca pods along the Widdershins Current. Packed lunch included.

## Midnight Bioluminescence Cruise

- Duration: 2 hours
- Price: £45 (ages 18 and over only)
- Vessel: The Wandering Molly
- Season: June–August
- A night sail through the glowing plankton blooms of Brine Sound. This tour is about bioluminescent plankton, not whales.

## Whale Song Silent Retreat

- Duration: 4 hours
- Price: £85 (ages 16 and over only)
- Vessel: The Wandering Molly
- Season: April–September
- Engines off, hydrophones down. Listen to whale song over the sound system in silence. No talking after we leave the harbour wall.
