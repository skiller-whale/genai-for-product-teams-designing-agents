# Frequently Asked Questions

## Where do I park?

The Long Stay car park on Kipper Lane is 5 minutes' walk from the harbour steps and does a £6 all-day rate for our customers — show your booking confirmation at the machine.
Do not park on the harbour wall itself; the fish market forklift has right of way and no brakes to speak of.

## What should I bring?

Layers, always layers. A waterproof, even in July. Sunglasses, sun cream, and a hat that ties on.
Binoculars if you have them (we hire them at £3 a trip if you don't).
Flat, grippy shoes — the deck is wetter than you think.

## I get seasick. Any tips?

Practical things that help: sail on a fuller stomach than you'd expect, stay on deck, watch the horizon, and stand midship where the boat moves least.
The Harbour Hop, in sheltered water, is the calmest choice.
For medication, please talk to a pharmacist or your GP — the crew can't advise on medicines.

## Can I buy a tour as a present?

Yes — gift vouchers from £10 to £500, valid for 24 months.
For an extra £2 we'll wrap the voucher in kelp-patterned paper and seal it with the company crest, which is a barnacle looking optimistic.

## What happens to lost property?

Handed in items live in the harbour office for one season, then go to the Port Brine lifeboat jumble.
Describe your item to lostandfound@barnacleandfluke.example and the harbourmaster will have a rummage.

## Do you guarantee whale sightings?

On qualifying tours, yes — see the Sighting Guarantee. In short: no whales on a qualifying tour, and everyone aboard gets a free re-sail voucher.

## Can I charter a whole boat?

The Salty Fluke and the Wandering Molly are available for private charter outside peak season — email the harbour office for rates.
