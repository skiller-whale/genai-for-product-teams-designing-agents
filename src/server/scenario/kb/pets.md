# Pets & Assistance Animals

## Pets

We love dogs. We do not allow them on our boats.
Whales and dogs have complicated feelings about each other, and our insurance has uncomplicated feelings about both.
Port Brine harbour office keeps a list of local dog-sitters and the Salty Paws day kennel on Wharf Street.

## Assistance animals

Trained assistance animals (guide dogs, hearing dogs, and other recognised assistance animals) are welcome aboard:

- **The Salty Fluke** (Harbour Hop)
- **Brinehilde** (Half-Day Humpback, Full-Day Orca Odyssey)

Both vessels have flat decks and sheltered areas suitable for a working animal, and the crew will provide fresh water.

**The Wandering Molly** cannot safely accommodate animals of any kind: she is a small historic sailing vessel with raised deck sills, open gunwales, and live rigging at deck level.
Where a customer with an assistance animal wishes to take a tour that sails on the Wandering Molly, we will happily offer the closest equivalent on another vessel (for example, the Half-Day Humpback in place of the Whale Song Silent Retreat) or a full refund.

Please let us know about an assistance animal at the time of booking so the crew can set up a comfortable spot.
