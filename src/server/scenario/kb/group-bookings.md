# Group & School Bookings

A group booking is any single booking for **10 or more people**.

## Rates

- Groups of 10 or more: 15% off the standard tour price for every member of the group.
- **School and youth groups** (accompanied groups from schools, colleges, scouts, guides and similar): **40% off** the standard adult price, on the **Harbour Hop** and **Half-Day Humpback** only. One accompanying adult sails free for every 10 young people.
- Group rates cannot be combined with membership discounts or promo codes.
- The Festival surcharge (20%) still applies to group bookings during Festival Week.

## Deposits

- Group bookings require a **25% deposit** at the time of booking.
- The balance is due 14 days before departure.

## Group cancellation schedule

Group bookings do **not** follow the standard refund ladder. Instead:

| Notice given | Refund |
|---|---|
| 28 days or more | 100% (including deposit) |
| 14–27 days | 50% |
| Less than 14 days | Deposit forfeited; any balance paid is refunded |

## Practicalities

- Groups larger than a vessel's capacity can be split across consecutive sailings.
- School groups must bring one adult per 10 young people (the free places above), plus any additional adults at the school rate.
- Risk-assessment paperwork for schools is available from the harbour office.
