# The Sighting Guarantee

We're proud of our sighting record, and we put our money where our blowhole is.

## The guarantee

If a qualifying tour sails and **no whales are sighted** during the trip, every customer on board receives a **free re-sail voucher**: one free place on the same tour, valid until the end of the current season (31 October).

## Qualifying tours

The guarantee applies to:

- Half-Day Humpback
- Full-Day Orca Odyssey
- Whale Song Silent Retreat

The guarantee does **not** apply to:

- **Harbour Hop** — a harbour wildlife tour; seals and seabirds are the stars, and whale sightings this close to shore are a bonus, not a promise.
- **Midnight Bioluminescence Cruise** — this tour is about the glowing plankton of Brine Sound, not whales, so whale sightings are never part of the deal.

## Small print

- "Sighted" means confirmed by the skipper or naturalist on board and recorded in the sailing log.
- Dolphins and porpoises, delightful as they are, do not count as whales for guarantee purposes.
- Re-sail vouchers are personal to the named customers on the booking and cannot be sold or transferred.
- Re-sail vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash.
