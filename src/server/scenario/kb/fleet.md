# Our Fleet

Three boats, three characters.

## The Salty Fluke

- Type: modern catamaran
- Capacity: 40 passengers
- Runs: Harbour Hop
- Wheelchair accessible; indoor saloon with café hatch.
- Named by public vote in 2011, narrowly beating "Boaty McWhaleface" (we don't talk about it).

## Brinehilde

- Type: purpose-built whale-watching cruiser
- Capacity: 60 passengers
- Runs: Half-Day Humpback, Full-Day Orca Odyssey
- Wheelchair accessible; heated observation deck; hydrophone array.
- The workhorse of the fleet and the skipper's favourite, though she denies having favourites.

## The Wandering Molly

- Type: restored 1928 wooden sailing ketch
- Capacity: 20 passengers
- Runs: Midnight Bioluminescence Cruise, Whale Song Silent Retreat
- Not wheelchair accessible (stepped gangway, raised deck sills).
- Sails under engine out of the harbour, then under canvas and silence.
