# Refund Policy (Customer Cancellations)

This policy applies when a **customer** cancels a booking.
If **we** cancel a sailing (for weather, safety, or mechanical reasons), the Weather & Safety Cancellations policy applies instead.

## Standard refund ladder

Refunds are calculated from the amount paid, based on how much notice is given before the scheduled departure:

| Notice given | Refund |
|---|---|
| 14 days or more | 100% |
| 7–13 days | 75% |
| 48 hours to 6 days | 50% |
| Less than 48 hours | 0% |

## Notes

- Notice is measured from the moment the cancellation request reaches us to the scheduled departure time.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days.
- Pod Members may be entitled to better terms than the standard ladder — see their membership tier benefits.
- Group bookings (10 or more people) follow a separate cancellation schedule — see Group Bookings.
- No-shows are treated as cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice.
- This policy covers cancellation before a tour. We do not offer satisfaction-based refunds after a tour has sailed; if a qualifying tour sees no whales, the Sighting Guarantee applies.
