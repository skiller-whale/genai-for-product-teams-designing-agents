# Seasonal Exceptions

Some weeks in Port Brine are not like the others.

## Festival Week (20–26 July)

During the Port Brine Whale Festival, demand for tours far exceeds capacity, and special terms apply to all sailings departing between 20 and 26 July inclusive:

- A **20% Festival surcharge** is added to all tour prices (after any membership or group discount is applied).
- **Promo codes are not valid** for sailings during Festival Week.
- Gift vouchers and re-sail vouchers remain valid as normal.
- Bookings during Festival Week cannot use the Dolphin tier free reschedule to move *into* Festival Week, only out of it.

## Migration Blackout (1–10 September)

The Full-Day Orca Odyssey does not run between 1 and 10 September, when the resident pods move through the Widdershins Narrows and boat traffic is restricted by the harbour authority.
Customers with affected bookings are contacted and rebooked under the Weather & Safety Cancellations policy, as a company-initiated cancellation.

## Winter Schedule (November–February)

Only the Harbour Hop runs in winter, weather permitting.
All other tours are out of season and cannot be booked for winter dates.
