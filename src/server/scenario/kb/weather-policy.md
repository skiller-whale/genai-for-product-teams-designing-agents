# Weather & Safety Cancellations

Port Brine weather is famously changeable, and our skippers have the final say on whether a sailing goes ahead.

## When we cancel

If the company cancels a sailing — for weather, sea state, safety, or mechanical reasons — every affected customer may choose either:

1. A **full refund** (100% of the amount paid), or
2. A **free rebooking** onto any equivalent sailing, plus a **20% discount voucher** for a future tour.

Pod Members at Orca tier get priority when rebooking after a weather cancellation.

## When we don't cancel

This policy applies **only when the company cancels the sailing**.
If a sailing is going ahead but a customer chooses not to sail — for example because the forecast looks unpleasant — that is a customer cancellation, and the standard Refund Policy ladder applies.

We know this feels harsh when the sky is grey.
Our skippers cancel whenever conditions are unsafe or sightings are impossible, and they make that call by 7am on the day of sailing (by 5pm the previous day for the Midnight Bioluminescence Cruise).

## How you'll hear

Cancellation notices go out by text message and email to the contact details on the booking, and are posted on the harbour board outside the ticket office.
